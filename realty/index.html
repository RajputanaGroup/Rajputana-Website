01 — The project

Elegance meets exquisite living.

More than a home — an elevated lifestyle, in one of Mumbai’s best-connected neighbourhoods.

Located in the vibrant heart of Goregaon West, Rajputana Residency offers a blend of modern living and timeless Mumbai charm. Each residence is designed with sleek lines, refined finishes and a sophisticated palette — a space that is both stylish and welcoming.

Business districts, schools, leading healthcare and premier shopping are all moments away. Excellent transport links make commuting simple, leaving more time for the lifestyle itself.
