Our story

Fewer bottles. Better ones.

New parents are handed a shelf of products and no way to tell them apart. Skwsh started from the opposite end — work out what a baby’s skin actually needs, name the ingredient that does it, and leave out everything that was only ever there for the label.

Every formula is built on clinical expertise and gentle enough for daily use from the very beginning. Baby care is where we started; it is not where we intend to stop.

Our mission

Simple, effective, nurturing products backed by clinical expertise — making the everyday routine easier and safer for the people doing it at 2am.

Our vision

A personal-care brand rooted in science and compassion that grows with the family, from newborn upward.
