Two brands, one home

Care and expression, kept in the same drawer.

Skin Fx grew out of a simple observation: most skincare is written for a life nobody has time for. So we made routines that flex — a mask that fits a Tuesday, a sunscreen that doesn’t argue with makeup.

Color Fx started at the other end, with colour as the fastest thing you can change about a day. Fast-drying, chip-resistant, toxin-free, and numbered so you can find your shade again.

Putting them under one name wasn’t a merger so much as an admission that people were already using them together.

Skin Fx

Care

Cleansers, sheet masks, sunscreen, wipes, hand and foot care.

Color Fx

Expression

Nail polish and nail care, including the Nail Fix range.

Our mission

Real skin, real life. We put science behind thoughtful design and build routines that flex with the day rather than demanding one.

Our vision

Everyday care that is personal, gentle and expressive — beauty that supports who you already are instead of asking you to change.
