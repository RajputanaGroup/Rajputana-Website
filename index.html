01 — About

Growing together, one route at a time.

A distributor is only as good as the relationships it keeps on both ends of the line — the principal who trusts us with a market, and the retailer who trusts us to turn up.

Rajputana began in 1982 as a pharmaceutical distributor working out of Mumbai, handling cartons for Cipla, Pfizer, Ranbaxy and Aventis. The business that grew from it kept the same discipline and applied it to progressively harder categories: fast-moving consumer goods, home care, personal care, baby care and telecom.

Somewhere along the way we made a deliberate choice to stop competing on volume alone. Moving to a value-based model meant fewer, better-matched principals and a route network built for premium segments — brands where shelf presentation, cold-chain discipline and chemist relationships matter more than raw tonnage.

Today the group spans carrying & forwarding, organised retail, multi-level marketing, realty and recruitment. The distribution arm still sits at the centre of it, and now carries our own cosmetics and baby-care brands alongside the principals we represent.
