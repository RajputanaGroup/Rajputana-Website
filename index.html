01 — Coverage

India is not one market. We cover the one that matters first.

Western India is the densest, most competitive retail corridor in the country, and it is where a brand’s India launch is won or lost. It has been our home market for four decades.

What we run, end to end

  1. 01
    Principal handoverImport documentation, GST, statutory labelling, MRP structuring.
  2. 02
    Carrying & forwardingStocking point, batch control, shelf-life discipline carried over from pharma.
  3. 03
    Secondary distributionOur own field team, beat plans and delivery into every serviced outlet.
  4. 04
    Channel executionGeneral trade, modern trade, chemist and e-commerce, each with its own commercial terms.
  5. 05
    Reporting backSecondary sales, outstanding and stock positions, from our in-house systems.

Our depth is in Maharashtra, where we run distribution out of Mumbai and Pune. From that base we have carried principals into carrying & forwarding mandates across western India and, for the right brand, nationwide.
