06 — Leadership

Three generations, one continuous business.

The founder built the network over four decades. The second generation rebuilt how it runs. The third is carrying it forward — a business that has never changed hands outside the family.

First generation

Late Shri Kirti Kanther

Founder · 1982–2025

confirm dates

The driving force behind the business from 1982, across distribution, carrying & forwarding, multi-level marketing, retail, realty, recruitment and agriculture — a resolute vision matched by unyielding leadership over four decades.

He was instrumental in launching well-known multinational brands across India, building not only a business but the community around it. Joint Secretary of the Goregaon Chapter, Jain International Trade Organisation, and its Chairman from 2020 to 2022.

Second generation

Pravesh K Kanther

B.Eng · MBA Finance · 2009

Joined in 2009 as the second generation, an engineering graduate with an MBA in finance. Has widened the principal portfolio and taken the business into modern trade, e-commerce and quick commerce.

Watched the sector shift from unorganised to organised and rebuilt the group’s systems around it — digitalised accounting, a field app for the sales team and an ordering app for retailers.

Third generation

Samarth Kanther

Business Management & Marketing · 2025

Joined the business in 2025 with a background in business management and marketing, bringing a tech-forward approach to a group that has spent four decades building the network he inherits.
